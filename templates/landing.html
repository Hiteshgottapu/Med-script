1. Multimodal Prescription Digitization

Upload raw clinic photos, handwritten scripts, or PDF reports. Our Google Cloud Vision Multimodal OCR engine automatically parses unstructured medical handwriting, maps medication names against clinical databases, and formats standard e-prescriptions.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about our AI models, pharmacy sources, Schedule H regulations, and data security.

Which AI models power MedScript?
MedScript utilizes Google Gemini 3.7 Flash (with fallback to 3.8 and 2.0 Flash) for conversational copilot assistance, Google Cloud Vision AI for multimodal prescription OCR, and Scikit-Learn (SVM + Random Forest) for symptom-disease prediction.
How does the medicine price comparison work?
When you search for a medicine, MedScript queries multiple verified sources in parallel (PharmEasy, OpenFDA NDC, NIH RxNorm). Our deduplication engine matches identical formulations and ranks results by lowest retail price and stock status.
What is Schedule H prescription compliance?
Under Indian Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, Schedule H medications require a valid registered medical practitioner's prescription. MedScript automatically detects Schedule H drugs and requires prescription document upload before checkout.
Is patient data private and secure?
Yes. MedScript complies with healthcare privacy standards. All transmissions are encrypted with 256-bit SSL, prescription uploads are isolated, and no health data is shared or sold to third parties.
